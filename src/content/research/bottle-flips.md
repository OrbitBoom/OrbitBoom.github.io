For my IB Mathematics AA HL Internal Assessment, I studied how the fill volume of a plastic bottle affected its chances of landing upright.

## From geometry to trials

I represented the bottle as a solid of revolution and studied its centre of mass. I then carried out 330 flips and used binomial distributions and confidence intervals to analyse the outcomes.

The highest observed success rate was 70%, at a 40% fill volume. That result applies to the bottle and conditions I tested.
