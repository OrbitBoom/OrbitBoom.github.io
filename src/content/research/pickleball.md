For my IB Mathematics Extended Essay, I investigated how the starting position on a pickleball court changes the choice of launch angle and velocity.

## Comparing trajectories

I used differential equations to derive parametric models for two cases, one with linear air resistance and one without. Python simulations let me vary the initial conditions and compare the resulting paths.

The comparison was between simulated trajectories. I didn’t measure real shots to validate the predictions.
