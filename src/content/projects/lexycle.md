I struggled to expand my Hindi vocabulary at school. For my IB Computer Science Internal Assessment, I built a way to keep the words I wanted to learn and return to them.

## Practising a word

Lexycle starts with a custom word list. Spaced-repetition flashcards bring words back for review. Multiple-choice quizzes and reading practice let me work with the same vocabulary in different ways. Game elements give the practice some structure.
